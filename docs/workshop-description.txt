[h1]Varied Pawns[/h1]

Two vanilla colonists of the same type are more alike than they should be. Skills land in a narrow band, passions land in a narrow band, and after a few colonies you stop being surprised by anyone.

Varied Pawns widens that band and hands you the dial.

[h2]What it does[/h2]

Every pawn rolls a single hidden [b]quality[/b] value when generated. That one roll drives three things together:

[list]
[*][b]Skill levels[/b] — shifted up or down from vanilla, with per-pawn noise on top
[*][b]Trait count[/b] — how many traits the pawn gets
[*][b]Passions[/b] — the budget the pawn spends on Minor and Major passions
[/list]

Because it is [i]one[/i] roll and not three, a pawn holds together: a lucky roll is good at things [i]and[/i] driven about them. An unlucky one is neither. You get colonists worth remembering instead of eight variations on the same person.

[b]It changes how many traits a pawn gets, not which ones are worth having.[/b] New traits come from vanilla's own picker, including every mod-added trait. When a pawn is over its target the mod drops one at random from the traits it is allowed to touch — traits forced by a xenotype, gene, backstory or scenario are never removed. Nothing here scores traits or plays favourites.

[h2]New in 1.1.0[/h2]

Master switches for skill, trait and passion variance, so you can hand any one of the three to another mod and keep the other two. Also fixes Profile Editor sliders staying draggable after a section was switched off.

[h2]Out of the box[/h2]

Install it and your colonists roll close to unmodded RimWorld, while the rest of the world stops being uniform. Raiders, tribes, imperial nobles and xenotypes come pre-assigned to profiles that suit them — a Sanguophage lord reads as a Sanguophage lord, and a tribal raid reads as scraped-together survivors.

Change any of it, or all of it, in the settings.

[h2]Eight presets[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Faithful[/b] — closest to unmodded RimWorld. Two to three traits, a vanilla passion budget, a narrow skill spread.
[*][b]Distinct[/b] — the mod's signature tuning. Strong individual strengths and weaknesses, with a fair colony average.
[*][b]Wildcard[/b] — maximum variation. Anywhere from 0 to 8 traits, zero or many passions, wide skill swings.
[*][b]Desperate[/b] — scraped-together survivors. Low skills, few passions, poor rolls common.
[*][b]Scavenger[/b] — wasteland survivors and pirates. Lower baseline skills, tough survival rolls.
[*][b]Specialist[/b] — engineered single-domain specialists such as Genies and Hussars. Focused skill spikes.
[*][b]Elite[/b] — imperial nobility and high-born. Consistently high capability.
[*][b]Sovereign[/b] — archite lords, Sanguophages, supreme leaders. Top-tier skills and wide passions.
[/list]

[b]Distinct and Wildcard are not power tiers.[/b] They sit around a normal average and spread pawns out around it — more range, not more strength. If you reroll a start until you like it, they reward you more than the tiers do, which is the point of them.

[h2]Build your own[/h2]

The Profile Editor gives you unlimited custom profiles. Every slider updates a live distribution curve and a power readout comparing the profile against vanilla, so you can see what a change actually does before you start a colony instead of finding out ten hours in.

New, Duplicate, Rename, Reset and Delete are all there. The eight presets are read-only — duplicate one to start from it.

[h2]Per-faction, per-race and per-xenotype overrides[/h2]

Assign different profiles to different pawns:

[list]
[*][b]Faction[/b] — the Empire gets Elite, pirates get Scavenger, tribes get Desperate. Ships with sensible defaults.
[*][b]Xenotype[/b] — Sanguophages get Sovereign, Hussars get Specialist, Impids get Wildcard. Requires Biotech.
[*][b]Race[/b] — works with Humanoid Alien Races mods. Ships empty, since your installed races are yours.
[/list]

Each override carries a priority from Lowest to Highest, so when a pawn matches more than one, the winner is the one you decided on rather than whichever rule happened to be checked first.

[h2]Sharing your setup[/h2]

Export your whole configuration — custom profiles, overrides, toggles — to the clipboard, and paste it back on another save or send it to a friend.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6[/b] only.
[*][b]Requires Harmony.[/b]
[*][b]Safe to add to an existing save.[/b] It affects newly generated pawns, so colonists you already have will not change — new arrivals, raiders and quest pawns will.
[*][b]Removing it mid-save is clean.[/b] The mod writes [i]nothing[/i] into your colony save — not one byte — so pulling it out leaves no orphaned data and no load errors. Your pawns keep everything they were given, because it lives on the pawn in RimWorld's own format.
[*][b]Biotech is optional.[/b] Without it the xenotype overrides are simply skipped; faction and race overrides work regardless.
[*][b]Tested at modpack scale.[/b] Runs clean inside a collection of over 1,300 mods — no load errors, no def conflicts, no Harmony collisions.
[*]Works with mod-added traits, skills, factions and races. It rides on RimWorld's own pawn generation rather than replacing it.
[/list]
